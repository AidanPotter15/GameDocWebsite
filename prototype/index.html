Cooler, fuel, upgrades

Cooler. Condition 0–100, starts at 100, drops 0.5 per travel hour. Cold life multiplies by condition/100. Below 25 the cooler is dead. Repair costs 4 hours and one Cooler Part, restores 30. Menu choice, no minigame.

Fuel. Tank 22, burns 1 per hour. Jerry Cans bulk 3, worth 10. Running dry ends the run at zero. Sell Jerry Cans at Home so fuel is a planning choice rather than a loot roll.

Upgrades. Score carries between runs. Three only: tank 22→30, cold 6→9, degrade 0.5→0.25.

Not in this prototype

  • Any 3D, any vehicle, any driving you perform
  • A repair minigame of any kind
  • Terrain, regions, world generation
  • Combat, enemies, threats
  • Engine parts or breakdowns beyond the cooler
  • Crafting or cooking
  • NPCs, story, setting, names
  • Art of any kind
  • Uncertainty about whether an item is really spoiled
