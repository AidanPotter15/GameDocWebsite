Decision log

What was decided and why. Read this before reopening an argument with yourself.

Settled

DecisionReason
Not voxelMinecraft's appeal is persistence and self-set goals, not blocks.
No wipes or runsThe whole reason the genre didn't fit. Most indie survival is run-based because it's cheaper.
Fixed terrain, procedural lootRoute planning is only a decision if distances are knowable. Random terrain makes it a reaction.
Vessel is fictional, not a real vehicleNo drivetrain knowledge required. Invented parts, authored failure rules.
Spoilage and cold chain as the coreDrawn from actual dietary work. Nothing in the genre models it.
Cooler degrades, not random failureVisible decline reads as dread. A surprise roll reads as unfair.
Fuel competes with cargo for hold spaceTurns route length into a cost you pay in the thing you drove out to get.
Jerry cans sold at HomeIf reaching the best location depends on a loot roll, players get stranded by luck. That's the version people hate.
Upgrades carry between runsOpens the far map by earning it instead of by tuning a constant.
Prototype in C#, text onlyInventory, timers, quantities, state. Same software already written before. Not an engine problem.
